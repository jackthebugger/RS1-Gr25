Component 2: Stakeholder Engagement Presentation
This assessment is your team’s opportunity to engage with your project stakeholder and communicate the value, direction and feasibility of your project.

Your goal is to give the stakeholder confidence that you understand their needs, have developed a meaningful robotics solution, and are on a credible path towards a useful final outcome.

Your tutor will assess the presentation from the perspective of the stakeholder you define, rather than primarily as a technical expert. This is therefore a stakeholder-facing presentation, but you should include enough technical substance, progress and evidence to demonstrate that your proposed system is feasible and that meaningful development is underway.

Using the provided presentation template, you will communicate:

who your stakeholder is;
the problem and how it is currently addressed;
the broader vision for your project;
your proposed semester solution;
the key technical challenges and your proposed approach;
how the system will work for the stakeholder;
evidence of progress so far;
what you intend to demonstrate by the end of the semester; and
your path to delivery, including key milestones and risks.


In-Class presentation
  You will deliver this presentation during the Week 6 Studio class.

  Your presentation is 10 minutes maximum. It is very important that you keep within the time limit; going over time will be penalised.

  All team members must be present in person and make a meaningful contribution to the presentation.

Criteria:
  Stakeholder Outcomes (How I feel as the client...)
    1) I clearly understand the problem and what you're proposing.

      * 3 Pts – Excellent: Crystal clear in plain English; I can explain it back.
      * 2 Pts – Proficient: Mostly clear; a few minor ambiguities.
      * 1 Pt – Marginal: Vague or jargony; key details unclear.
      * 0 Pts – Not Evident: I don't understand the project.

    2) I believe this will deliver real value for me.

      * 3 Pts – Excellent: Strong relevance; clear and compelling benefits.
      * 2 Pts – Proficient: Relevant; benefits are reasonably clear.
      * 1 Pt – Marginal: Weak link to my needs; benefits unclear.
      * 0 Pts – Not Evident: No convincing value.

    3) This project stands out to me.

      * 3 Pts – Excellent: Creative, distinctive and memorable; goes beyond the obvious.
      * 2 Pts – Proficient: Thoughtful, with some distinctive elements.
      * 1 Pt – Marginal: Mostly conventional or generic.
      * 0 Pts – Not Evident: No clear originality or project identity.

    4) I'm convinced by the progress shown.

      * 3 Pts – Excellent: Meaningful progress; something real is working or has been tested, with convincing evidence.
      * 2 Pts – Proficient: Credible evidence; implementation or testing is underway.
      * 1 Pt – Marginal: Mostly claims, basic setup, or limited evidence.
      * 0 Pts – Not Evident: No credible evidence of progress.

    5) I trust your path to the final demo.

      * 3 Pts – Excellent: Clear final outcome, next steps and key risks; I believe you can deliver.
      * 2 Pts – Proficient: Credible plan; next steps and risks mostly clear.
      * 1 Pt – Marginal: High-level intentions; path or risks are vague.
      * 0 Pts – Not Evident: No credible path to delivery.

    6) I felt engaged as the stakeholder.

      * 3 Pts – Excellent: Clear, concise, engaging, and on time; convincing explanations.
      * 2 Pts – Proficient: Generally clear; minor pacing or jargon issues.
      * 1 Pt – Marginal: Hard to follow or poorly pitched to me.
      * 0 Pts – Not Evident: Not understandable / over time.

    Total points: 18
