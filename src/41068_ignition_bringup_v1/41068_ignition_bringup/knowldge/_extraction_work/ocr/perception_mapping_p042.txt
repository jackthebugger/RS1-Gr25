Conclusions
> Robots typically have many different sensors
> Robot mapping is the process by which a robot builds a representation of its environment
using data from its sensors (and prior information).
> Sensors and maps are useful for:
¢ Enabling autonomy: Understand location of obstacles, landmarks, traversability, navigable areas
« Primary tasks: Provide information to humans and other robots that is specific to the task. May be
provided in a user interface.
> Many different types of maps for different purposes:
* Occupancy, terrain, feature, topological, semantic, environmental, ... maps
> Occupancy grid mapping fuses data from multiple noisy observations
