Configuration Space

> Although the motion planning problem is defined in the regular world (the “workspace’”),
it lives in another space: the configuration space

> Arobot configuration q is a specification of the position of all robot points relative to a
fixed coordinate system

> Usually a configuration is expressed as a vector of positions and orientations

> The configuration space (“C-space”) is the space of all possible configurations

> The topology of C-space is usually not the Cartesian space
