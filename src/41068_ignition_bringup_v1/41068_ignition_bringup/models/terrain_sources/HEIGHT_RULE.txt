HEIGHT_RULE for 03_colour_surface_map.png (and visual interpretation of 02)

Deep green grass / open forest floor / central corridor: LOW → dark grey in heightmap
Light green grass / sparse vegetation: LOW–MID
Tan dirt roads and clearings: LOW → LOW–MID
Brown/grey rocky outcrops and broad raised ground: MID–HIGH
Pale grey rock / upper ridge areas: HIGH → light grey in heightmap
Blue water: LOWEST → near-black in heightmap
Dense tree canopy: ZERO additional ground height; canopy is visual surface detail only
Buildings/structures: Treat as surface/layout features rather than terrain elevation
  unless their footprint corresponds to an existing raised area

The low east–west traversable corridor runs broadly across the middle of the map,
with the west-centre portion deliberately flattened to provide the robot spawn lowland.
The highest terrain is concentrated around the broad upper-right/north-east raised area,
with no extreme peak or abrupt elevation change.
