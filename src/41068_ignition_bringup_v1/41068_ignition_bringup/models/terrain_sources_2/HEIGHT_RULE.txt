HEIGHT_RULE for 03_colour_surface_map.png

Blue water = LOWEST elevation.
Tan/brown maintained trail = LOW to LOW-MID, relatively flat and smooth.
Dark/medium green bushland = MID elevation with gentle rolling variation.
Lighter green clearing = LOW to MID.
Grey/brown rocky areas = MID-HIGH to HIGH.
Pale exposed rocky rises = HIGH.
Vegetation canopy adds no additional ground elevation.
Buildings/structures are surface/layout features, not terrain elevation.

Terrain locations: The primary low/traversable corridor follows the broad
connected trail network through the centre of the map, including the west/left-centre
clearing used as the preferred flat spawn region.
Highest terrain: The highest rolling ground is concentrated mainly toward the
eastern/right-central side, with additional raised terrain around the outer
bushland rather than isolated mountain peaks.
